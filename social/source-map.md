## Safe reusable answers

- **How old is ROB?** The current treaded prototype dates to about 2016, with
  surviving ROB-specific software evidence from 2017–2018 onward. ROB has been
  rebuilt repeatedly rather than completed in one moment.
- **Is ROB autonomous?** ROB combines sensors, controllers, and experimental
  higher-level software, but public motion is operator-supervised and bounded by
  safety controls. Do not imply general or human-level autonomy.
- **What runs ROB?** The documented stack spans a Mac running Cerebro, Arduino
  firmware for base control, Apple-platform controllers, cameras, lidar, and
  experimental perception/AI components. Name only the components relevant to
  the question.
- **Can I build a copy?** The books teach systems, mechanics, circuits, control,
  testing, and safety. They are not a dimensioned plan for copying a heavy robot.
- **Why so many exposed parts?** ROB is an evolving prototype. Open panels and
  workshop photos make routing, service access, iteration, and unresolved work
  visible.
