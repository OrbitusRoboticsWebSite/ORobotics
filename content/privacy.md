# Orbitus Robotics App Privacy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

ROB Training and ROB Training Vision do not collect, track, sell, or share personal data. The apps do not contain advertising, third-party analytics, user accounts, or in-app purchases.

Game progress and high scores are stored locally on your device. The apps do not upload gameplay, scores, or device identifiers to Orbitus Robotics.

ROB Voice is optional. Microphone access begins only after you tap the voice control and approve Apple's system permission. Speech recognition is required to run on device, and supported Apple Intelligence responses are generated on device. Orbitus Robotics does not receive or store microphone audio, transcripts, or generated responses.

The iPhone and iPad AR Lab requests camera access only when you open the AR experience. Camera images are used to place simulated ROB content in your environment and are not transmitted to Orbitus Robotics.

You can revoke microphone, speech-recognition, or camera access at any time in your device's Settings app. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

Questions about these practices can be sent to [orbitus@orbitusrobotics.com](mailto:orbitus@orbitusrobotics.com). Learn more about Orbitus Robotics at [www.orbitusrobotics.com](https://www.orbitusrobotics.com/).
