## Create a movie wall

1. In Musimotion, open **New Presentation → Movie Walls** and select a design. **Robot Cutting Room** pairs a wide main film with two stacked details. **Robot Panorama Wall** positions three films in a row at different depths. **Robot Mission Triptych** brings three close-ups into view in sequence.
2. For the bundled version, choose **Create Presentation** and press **Play**. Its three short reels use silent excerpts. Each film is an editable video layer with its own framing and motion.
3. To use complete films, select **Use all 19 full Orbitus robot films (97 MB download)** before creating the presentation. Keep the gallery open as the film count advances. Musimotion verifies the files and embeds them across seven scenes, up to three panels per scene. If a download fails, reconnect and choose **Create Presentation** again.

   *An actual Vision Pro simulator download using the public Orbitus films. The counter advances as the files are verified.*

4. On Vision Pro, choose **Open Volume** to see the panels at different depths, or **Full Immersion** to enter the presentation. Use the player to pause and inspect the framing. On a Mac or iPad, open the same saved presentation and use the timeline to edit each movie layer.
