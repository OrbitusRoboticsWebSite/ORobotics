## Create a movie wall

1. In Musimotion, open **New Presentation → Movie Walls** and select a design. **Robot Cutting Room** sets two details beside a wide film. **Robot Panorama Wall** brings three screens from a wide row into close-ups. **Robot Mission Triptych** brings the pictures through the foreground on a diagonal.
2. Choose **Create Presentation** and press **Play**. The outdoor Maker Faire drive takes the foreground first, the indoor droid display follows, then ROB turns with a can in the workshop. Each screen approaches, fills the view and sweeps aside. The development templates play these complete 2026 films with an original electronic score.
3. To use complete films, select **Use all 19 full Orbitus robot films (97 MB download)** before creating the presentation. Keep the gallery open as the film count advances. Musimotion verifies the files and embeds them across seven scenes. The first scene uses the original full-height Maker Faire and torso-turn films. The next five pair the early workshop films with recycling or pouring footage. The closing scene plays the vertical recycling reel. If a download fails, reconnect and choose **Create Presentation** again.

4. On Vision Pro, choose **Open Volume** to see the panels at different depths, or **Full Immersion** to enter the presentation. Use the player to pause and inspect the framing. On a Mac or iPad, open the same saved presentation. In **Objects**, select **Maker Faire - Outdoor Drive**, **Maker Faire - Droid Hall** or **Workshop - Chassis Turn** to edit that panel. The source movie, pan and zoom inside its frame, stage position, depth and scale belong to that layer.
