ROB returned to **Maker Faire Bay Area, September 25–27, 2026**, at the historic
Mare Island Naval Shipyard in Vallejo, California.

This album starts at the Orbitus Robotics booth: ROB beneath the canopy, with
the robot-building and training posters behind it. The weekend continues with
droid displays, a moment between exhibits, and a colorful visitor outside the
booth. Scroll on for Kierie's closer look at Sunday's demonstrations and the
busy exhibition hall.

{{< foldergallery src="2026_MakerFaire" title="ROB at Maker Faire Bay Area 2026" initial="8" >}}

On **Sunday, September 27**, ROB ventured beyond the booth. A blue lightsaber
rose above its articulated arms as it traveled through the hall, stopped beside
R2-D2 and the other droids, and drew visitors in for a closer look. The rear and
side views reveal ROB's camera head, articulated arms, tracked base, and the
hardware that makes the demonstrations possible.

Kierie's photos also take in the colorful rovers at the HomeBrew Robotics Club
display. The two short clips show ROB among the droids and rolling outside the
Orbitus canopy. They play at normal speed without audio; the caption and
transcript controls provide descriptions of the visible action.
