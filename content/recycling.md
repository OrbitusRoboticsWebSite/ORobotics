An ordinary aluminum can gives ROB plenty to practice: reaching, lining up the
gripper, holding on, and letting go. These recent workshop sessions bring all
of that work into view.

The videos gather short moments from supervised tests. Long waits and
setup time have been cut out. It is a look at a prototype in development;
the montage does not measure reliability or show a continuous autonomous run.
