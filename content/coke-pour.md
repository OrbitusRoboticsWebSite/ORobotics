The pour is here. Watch the wrist turn, the stream reach the glass, and the can
come back upright.

This is a supervised prototype test with a helping hand. A person catches and
repositions the glass, and there is a spill. Those moments stay in the edit:
we are still refining the motion and alignment.

The 31-second reel keeps two excerpts from the same take at normal speed.
Long waits are removed. The sound comes from the original workshop recording.
