Make a title sequence, add a location stamp, paint a Sumi title to music or roll credits toward a vanishing point. The ten [black dragon paintings](#paint-a-black-dragon) honor the HeiLongWuShu school. Each has its own shamisen score and tempo. You can [extend an opening and keep the same music playing into the next scene](#extend-a-scene-and-its-soundtrack). The template supplies editable text, shapes and keyframes. Use the updated development preview on each device for the painted strokes and perspective lettering. The screenshots show the running app with synthetic demonstration content. The poster sheets use frames rendered from the editable projects. Select a picture to see the controls at full size.

## Start with a title

1. In the library, choose **Create**. On Mac you can also choose **File → New Presentation…** or press **⌘N**. In the Vision Pro spatial workspace, choose **Project → New Presentation…**.

2. Choose **Titles**, then **Cinematic Title**. The outline marks the selected poster. Each poster shows a frame from the presentation. Choose **All** to browse every category. Use **Search templates** to find a name, category or word in its description. For example, type “crawl” to find Starfield Crawl. If you see **No matching templates**, choose **Clear search** and **All**. For a clean canvas choose **Blank Presentation**. For the familiar title and orb, select **Original Canvas** under **All**.

## Add a second scene

5. With the title presentation open, choose **Templates → Lower Thirds → Location Stamp**. Choose **Import into Presentation** to append the location scene. For a separate project, choose **Open as New Presentation**, which saves the current work in the library before opening the template.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/lower-third-selected.webp" "Lower Thirds category with Location Stamp selected above Import into Presentation" >}}

*The editor’s template chooser offers both actions beneath the selected lower third.*

6. Play the imported scene. The location stamp settles into the lower right. Select **Place name** and **Coordinates** in **Objects** to change the two lines individually. Import adds a whole scene with its own background and duration. Choose **Undo** to remove the last import.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/imported-scene.webp" "Objects for the imported North Coast scene with independently selectable place name and coordinates" >}}

*The new scene joins the title sequence. Its place name, coordinates and backing can be edited separately.*

## Animate one layer

7. In a scene, select a visible layer and move the playhead to the moment its motion should begin. Open **Effects → Motion** in the artwork editor. In Vision Pro’s 3D Setup workspace, choose **Effects → Motion Presets**. Choose **Entrance**, **Accent** or **Exit** from the group menu, then set **Duration** to **0.5 s**, **1 s** or **2 s**. Click a preset to apply it immediately.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/motion-entrance.webp" "Motion tab showing Entrance and Duration menus above three motion presets" >}}

*Rise & Settle moves the selected layer upward, lets it overshoot slightly and brings it to rest.*

| Group | Preset | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entrance | Rise & Settle, Elastic Reveal, Focus Pull | Rise and land, stretch open horizontally, or sharpen from soft focus. |
| Accent | Heartbeat, Pendulum, Orbit | Pulse twice, rock side to side, or travel a loop and return. |
| Exit | Sweep Away, Fold Down, Tumble Out | Fade to the right, collapse vertically, or turn and shrink while falling. |

In **3D Volume**, a blur keyframe makes Musimotion draw text or a shape on a flat surface at that layer’s animated depth. For a solid shape that keeps its thickness, use a transform preset such as **Rise & Settle** or **Pendulum**.

8. Read the time range under the menus and choose **Play from Start** to see the result. On Vision Pro, previewing from 3D Setup returns you to the artwork editor and starts playback. The range uses seconds from the start of the current scene. If the layer ends early, Musimotion fits the preset into its remaining time. Each application adds ordinary layer keyframes to the project. In the inspector, open **Keyframes** and choose a timestamp. Adjust a property such as **Rotation** or **Opacity** at that pose, then play again. **Undo** immediately after applying a preset removes the entire application in one step.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/motion-applied.webp" "Rise and Settle time range and Play from Start in the Motion tab" >}}

*The applied preset shows its name, actual time range and playback control.*

9. If the presets are dimmed, select a visual layer and move the playhead inside its visible span. Leave at least one frame before its end. The panel then enables the presets. Audio tracks can be timed in the timeline.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/motion-unavailable.webp" "Motion presets dimmed with the instruction to move the playhead before the end of the layer" >}}

*At the end of the layer the panel explains where to move the playhead.*

## Paint a title to music

10. Choose **Create → Sumi Ink → Circle of Breath → Create Presentation**. Choose **Go to Start** and **Play**. A dark round stroke begins the circle. A finer dry-brush stroke joins on the third beat, while the opening line and title appear on the paper.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/sumi-category.webp" "Sumi Ink filter with Circle of Breath selected among illustrated paper and ink presentations" >}}

*The Sumi Ink category includes circle, bamboo, tide, blossom, mountain and reed studies. Choosing a poster shows its description.*

11. Open **Objects** and select **Title** to replace “Breath”. **Opening line** and **Subtitle** are separate text layers. To change the paint, select a **Live Paint** stroke. Increase **Water** for a softer edge, change **Ink Load** for denser or lighter pigment, then scrub across the entrance to see the change. If the first frame looks empty, advance the playhead.

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/sumi-catalog.webp" "Six rendered paintings of a brush circle, bamboo, tides, plum blossoms, blue mountains and dusk reeds with their sample title lettering" >}}

*Top to bottom, the pairs show Circle of Breath and Bamboo in Rain, Tide Ledger and Plum Blossom, then Blue Mountain and Reeds at Dusk. In Blue Mountain the character 山 (mountain) is painted in three strokes over the ridge. Play from three seconds to see their order. Each picture is a rendered frame from its own project.*

{{< musimotion-capture "video-templates/sumi-objects.webp" "Circle of Breath in the editor with Open Circle audio and individual Live Paint strokes in Objects" >}}

*Objects exposes the native layers, individual brush gestures and Open Circle audio track. Select Live Paint · Sumi Round to edit the first stroke.*

12. In the timeline, select the **Open Circle** audio track. The original chamber score plays at 68 BPM over three bars. Mute the track to compare the motion in silence, then unmute it and replay. The drawing times and the score travel in the same presentation package.

## Paint a black dragon

Ten Sumi ink paintings honor the HeiLongWuShu school. Each unfolds to four bars of original shamisen music. For a quiet opening, **Lotus Stillness** takes 16 seconds at 60 beats per minute (BPM). For a brisk finish, **Dawn Homecoming** covers the same four bars in just over seven seconds at 136 BPM.

16. Choose **Create → Black Dragons → The Ink Gate → Create Presentation**. If you already have a project open, choose **Templates → Black Dragons → The Ink Gate → Import into Presentation** to add the painting as a scene. To find the collection from **All**, enter “HeiLongWuShu” or “shamizan” in **Search templates**.

## Extend a scene and its soundtrack

The Ink Gate paints its dragon over a four-bar shamisen phrase. At 64 BPM, that phrase lasts 15 seconds. Give the dedication another 7.5 seconds on screen and choose whether the music continues under it.

1. Open **The Ink Gate**, choose **Objects**, then select the opening scene under **Scenes**. Choose **Scene Timing…**. In the Vision Pro spatial workspace, select the scene and choose **Project → Scene Timing…**.

{{< musimotion-capture "black-dragons/timing-open.webp" "The Ink Gate selected in Objects, with Scene Timing beneath the scene name and the original 15-second range" >}}

*The Scenes list shows the opening ends at 15 seconds. Choose the timer just below its name.*

2. Enter **22.5** in **Duration (seconds)**. Choose **Apply** to change just the scene length. The finished painting now holds until 22.5 seconds. Its brushstrokes and title keyframes still run at their original pace. With **Continue soundtrack** off, the shamisen finishes at 15 seconds.

{{< musimotion-capture "black-dragons/timing-duration.webp" "Scene Timing showing the duration field for The Ink Gate" >}}

*The iPad simulator shows the opening at its original 15 seconds, with the soundtrack set to play once. Replace 15 with 22.5 to hold the dedication for another two bars.*

3. To fill that extra time, open **Scene Timing…** again. Turn on **Continue soundtrack**, check the track name **The Ink Gate · Shamisen**, then choose **Apply**. If the project has several audio tracks, choose one from **Track**. The phrase repeats from its beginning at 15 seconds. If you used a trimmed audio clip, its selected portion repeats.

{{< musimotion-capture "black-dragons/timing-soundtrack.webp" "Scene Timing with Continue soundtrack enabled for The Ink Gate’s shamisen score" >}}

*The iPad simulator shows 22.5 seconds and the switch on. Choose Apply to keep the named track playing after the original four bars.*

4. Choose **Templates → Lower Thirds → Location Stamp → Import into Presentation** to add a scene. Start playback shortly before the cut at 22.5 seconds. You will hear the phrase carry on halfway through its second pass as the location appears. Adding another scene or changing its duration moves the end of the music automatically. Save the project to keep this choice. The movie export uses the same timing. Share the exported movie with viewers using an earlier version of Musimotion so they hear the continued score. Earlier editors play the original clip once.

{{< musimotion-capture "black-dragons/timing-held.webp" "The finished ink dragon and dedication remain on screen 19 seconds into the extended opening" >}}

*On the iPad simulator, the transport has passed 19 seconds. The dragon and dedication remain in view beyond their original 15-second opening.*

In the source scene, the audio row covers the complete continuing range. A later scene shows the continuing track on its own loop row. Select that row to return to the source scene and edit its audio.

To return to a single play, turn off **Continue soundtrack** and choose **Apply**. The original audio keeps its authored start and end. If **Apply** is dimmed after a time edit, enter a number of at least 0.1 seconds. Shortening the scene skips anything beyond the new end. Extend it again to reveal those later events.

If the switch is dimmed because the project has no audio track, add a score with **Music Studio → Generate Music → Add to Scene**, then reopen **Scene Timing…**. If a later template brings its own music, both tracks can sound together. Use **Mute Audio Track** on the later score to hear only the continuing one.
