Apple Books release path

Publish only what has passed the gate.

Apple requires a validated EPUB, cover, sample, metadata, and separate rights and pricing. Orbitus adds human review before any ROB edition goes live.

Review and rights

Close editorial placeholders; review youth safety, technical claims, photograph rights, privacy, trademark use, and accessibility.

Validate the edition

Build an accessible EPUB 3 with live searchable text, run EPUBCheck, inspect it on iPhone and iPad, and verify every image description, sample, and link.

Release and connect

Submit through the Apple Books publishing portal, set rights and pricing, group the numbered volumes as a series, then add each final product URL here.
