Coordinated tread control

Drive, solve, and defend

On phones and touch screens, the two ROBController-style joysticks directly command the matching left and right treads; hold both forward to advance, pull one back to pivot, and release either stick to stop that tread. Keyboard players can use WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space for the equipped melee weapon. The selected ranged weapon scans continuously and turns the lock indicator red when it acquires a target. Tap Q for a quick projectile or hold it for a charged shot. Walls stop projectiles and melee attacks, so reposition until the lane is clear.
