Coordinated tread control

Drive, solve, and defend

On phones and touch screens, the two ROBController-style joysticks directly command the matching left and right treads; hold both forward to advance, pull one back to pivot, and release either stick to stop that tread. Keyboard players can use WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space for alternating wide, dual-arm saber sweeps; chain three attacks to extend both sabers and spin ROB's torso. The black right-shoulder gatling scans continuously and turns red when locked. Tap Q for a quick shot or hold it for a larger, deeper blast.
