Coordinated tread control

Drive, solve, and defend

On phones and touch screens, the two ROBController-style joysticks directly command the matching left and right treads; hold both forward to advance, pull one back to pivot, and release either stick to stop that tread. Keyboard players can use WASD or the arrow keys. Press Space for the equipped melee weapon. The pan-and-tilt mount scans with a red virtual targeting dot, then fires a blue training bolt at simulated targets. Tap Q for a quick shot or hold it to charge; ranged tools draw from the same system energy as the treads. Walls stop projectiles and melee attacks, so reposition until the lane is clear.

Laser safety boundary

The red targeting dot and blue “balloon popper” are colorful game effects only. Circuit Quest does not provide real high-power laser construction details. Never aim a real laser at a person, animal, vehicle, aircraft, or reflective surface; use only age-appropriate, supervised products exactly as labeled.
