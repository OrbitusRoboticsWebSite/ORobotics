How to read this series

Start with the motion-transition study, then the controller compatibility audit and recovery plan. The fourth paper examines an older X-Hub computer as a dual-arm development candidate. Each paper introduces ROB’s purpose and software context, distinguishes observations from proposals, and identifies the physical measurements still needed.

The fifth paper examines SignalAtlas, BlackViper, Apple Endpoint Security, and the next generation of LLM and machine-learning-assisted threat investigation. Its competitive analysis separates implemented capabilities from proposed integrations.

Robotics photos come from Kierie’s October 29 can-handling videos. They document earlier work with ROB; they do not show the replacement controller operating. Security diagrams are original conceptual illustrations.

These are Orbitus Robotics technical white papers, not peer-reviewed journal articles. Each edition includes its source commits, limitations, and an AI-assistance disclosure.
