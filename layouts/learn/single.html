A progression that keeps returning to evidence

Observe. Model. Test. Explain. Revise.

The classroom gets more precise without changing its core habits. Learners always separate energy from information, observations from authority, and a successful demo from a verified system.

01

See the system

Classify inputs, outputs, structure, energy, signals, feedback, and safe states with virtual circuits and paper models.

02

Control the model

Use the games to experience differential drive, timing, collisions, energy budgets, upgrades, sensors, and mission planning.

03

Read the implementation

Follow the books and source into protocols, control ownership, measured motion, perception limits, privacy, and bounded autonomy.
