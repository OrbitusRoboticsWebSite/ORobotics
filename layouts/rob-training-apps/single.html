iPhone and iPad

Mission control, AR Lab, and ROB Voice

The iOS app combines a RealityKit campaign with an AR Lab and a component explorer. Place ROB on a detected surface, inspect its animated tri-wheel tread base, power, Cerebro, sensors, safety systems, and dual arms, then ask the on-device ROB guide questions about the mission.

  • Automatic differential-tread mixing
  • Keys, doors, cells, attacking enemies, dual-saber combos, and charged shoulder lasers
  • Local high score and procedural soundtrack
  • No physical-robot control connection

Apple Vision Pro

A spatial robotics workshop

The visionOS app presents the complete campaign in a glass mission window, then opens an immersive workshop where ROB can be placed, scaled, driven, inspected, and operated with spatial controls or a connected keyboard.

  • Fifteen-level campaign visible at a glance
  • Full-scale RealityKit ROB model
  • Spatial movement, saber combos, shoulder laser, objectives, and component learning
  • Speech questions and local generated techno

Build it and learn from it

Swift and RealityKit developers can follow the shared campaign model, native rendering, spatial interface, speech features, procedural audio, and separate iOS and visionOS entry points in the public repository.
