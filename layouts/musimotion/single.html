AI Creation Studio Apple Intelligence

One prompt becomes a production-ready starting point.

Apple’s Foundation Models framework privately plans concise copy, timing, layout, palette, and camera movement on the device. Image Playground lets you review or refine the artwork before it enters the scene. Musimotion then builds the result from ordinary text, image, and animation layers you can move, rewrite, retime, or remove.

  4. 04Edit everythingEvery generated layer and keyframe remains a normal Musimotion object.
