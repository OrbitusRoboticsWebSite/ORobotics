Your original controls.
A volume with real depth.

Read existing 2D presentations in the familiar illustrated editor. Choose 3D Volume to animate solid text, shapes and ink behind and in front of the canvas. Mac and iPad render the same saved project through a movable camera.

On Vision Pro, Open Volume creates a bounded spatial window. Full Immersion surrounds you with the presentation and keeps the original turquoise playback controls within reach. Exit 3D pauses playback and returns to the editor.

Build a 3D music video from 32 editable designs. Beat Rail Express sends a modeled train along angled tracks, with overhead poles and moving traffic on separate depth planes. Each carriage crosses the signal on a beat. Chroma Nova opens into a colored starburst. Five spherical panoramas surround the viewer with an original environment. On Vision Pro, choose your own audio file to repeat the visual phrase for the whole song.

Open 3D Models to animate the photographic ROB scan and its neon callouts, or record poses for the eleven-joint ROB display rig. Watch the three model tours and try the downloadable URDF.

Click an object in the Mac 3D view to edit it, or open Objects to find it by name. Choose from fifty authored templates for animated titles, lower thirds, charts and spatial scenes. The ten HeiLongWuShu tribute paintings pair black dragons in Sumi ink with original shamisen scores. You can also send your own credits toward a vanishing point. Import more scenes as you edit. Extend an opening and continue its music across a scene change. Apply an entrance, accent or exit to any visual layer. See the template and motion walkthrough. The timeline scrubber has its own row below the playback controls.

Music Studio generates music for your presentation on Mac, iPad and Vision Pro. Choose an Electronic, Studio Band or Chamber ensemble, or choose a dedicated Retro 8-bit or Shamisen arrangement. Preview a variation before adding it to a scene. Rhythm Studio can turn the accents in that performance into an editable target chart.

Droid Arena turns a Vision Pro scene into three waves of flying droids. In Full Immersion a pulse tool follows your right hand. Reach toward your left shoulder to draw the shield, or use the shield control in front of you.

Rhythm Studio turns your song into a playable chart. Choose a hand, lane and shape for each target. In Full Immersion, a 360° panorama surrounds you as you cut shapes to the music with two hand-tracked sabers.

Import the Birthday Surprise template to add a countdown and greeting, falling balloons, confetti and fireworks. Adjust fall speed, wind and bounce. Mac and iPad show the saved camera view. On Vision Pro, the balloon drop surrounds you and can react to your tracked position.
