01 / COMPOSE

Text, shapes, symbols, photos, and video

Build layered compositions from familiar materials. Scale each axis independently, position precisely, and keep the canvas clean until you need a control.

02 / ANIMATE

Motion that belongs to time

Set keyframes, tune layer spans, and play the sequence from a centralized transport designed to make timing feel natural.

03 / PAINT

Brushes that can perform live

Draw with raster paint or turn strokes into live particles. Color, motion, and touch become part of the presentation instead of decoration around it.

Let the scene surprise you

Give elements mass, gravity, and collision behavior with SpriteKit-powered physics, then combine authored movement with responsive performance.
