Report from any platform.

A provisioned Mac, Windows PC, or Linux machine sends signed HTTPS JSON directly to CloudKit. No Cloudflare account or Apple sign-in is needed on the reporting machine.

  1. Download the reporter and use Node.js 22 or later.
  2. Get a private reporting profile and dedicated signing key from your administrator.
  3. Fill in status.json, validate it, and send. The reporter supplies your device ID and the current time.
